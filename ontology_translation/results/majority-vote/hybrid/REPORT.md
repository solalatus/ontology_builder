## Caveats

- The hold-out is one domain (itops), older prompts and low-recall interviews: a different regime
  from the current multi-domain runs.
- Heuristic scoring only (wording-based); the semantic judge is phase 2 of #174.
- A fully clean test would use fresh multi-domain interviews (new runs).
- Relationship precision and recall are both near the floor on the hold-out (old, low-recall interviews
  against 108 gold relationships), so it tests the relationship vote in a regime where relationships carry
  little signal.

## Deviation from the design

HYBRID-DESIGN.md says "eleven sets" and "at least 9 of the 11", but its own table lists 10 sets: the
count in the text was wrong. The table is what was run, and the threshold was kept at 9 wins (now out of
10, which is stricter). The verdict does not depend on it: the hybrid beat union in 0 of 10 sets.
